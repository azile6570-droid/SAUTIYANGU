Privacy by design

Your conversations belong to you.

  • Messages are encrypted before upload.
  • Never publish a phone number publicly.
  • Users can block or report anyone.
  • Calls require camera and microphone permission.

This school prototype demonstrates the interface. Real online messaging and calls require a secure backend and WebRTC signaling server.
